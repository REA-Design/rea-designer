A logo alone usually takes 3-5 business days, while a complete visual identity (logo + cards + applications) takes 1-2 weeks depending on the project's scope.

Files are delivered in multiple formats depending on use: PNG and JPG for quick use, PDF for printing, and an editable source file (AI/PSD) for future edits.
